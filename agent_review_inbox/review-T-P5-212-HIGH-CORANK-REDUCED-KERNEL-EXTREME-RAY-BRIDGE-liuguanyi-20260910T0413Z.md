# T-P5-212 — high-corank reduced-kernel extreme-ray bridge

## 0. Verdict and seam closed

**CONDITIONAL_PASS_MATHEMATICAL_CHILD / pending source binding.**

T-P5-211 solves the branch in which both the active Schur anchor and the reduced Schur remainder have corank one. Its Boundary B explicitly leaves the case where the reduced PSD Schur block `K` has higher corank, because `adj(K)=0` then destroys the two-scalar packet.

This review closes that reduced-block gap while deliberately keeping the already-solved active anchor assumptions of T-P5-211. The main result is:

> For an arbitrary PSD reduced block `K`, the cone `ker(K) ∩ R_+^p` is generated by finitely many support-minimal rays, and each such ray is exactly a strict-positive kernel of a corank-one principal block `K_SS`. After the common-zero first-order debit condition is imposed, only the zero-loaded rays survive. Pulling the debit quadratic onto those surviving generators turns the entire higher-corank T-P5-210 witness search into one ordinary orthant quadratic problem.

Under the actual T-P5-210 assumption that the debit matrix `Q` is copositive and the reference state is a common zero, the pulled matrix is itself automatically copositive. Therefore existence of a positive second-order debit witness is even cheaper than a generic copositivity query: it is equivalent to the pulled matrix being **nonzero**. In the absence of the inherited `Q`-copositivity theorem, the exact generic criterion is `-M0` not copositive.

The result is a mathematical bridge only. It does not establish actual source identity, selector/cell/tube coverage, a concrete P5 candidate, Float64/runtime semantics, Lean/kernel validation, independent verification, registry admission, or parent closure.

## 1. Upstream Schur setup retained from T-P5-211

Let the endpoint quadratic on critical variables `(u,eta)` be

`H = [[A, R^T], [R, C]]`,

with `u in R^m` signed and `eta in R_+^p` one-sided. Assume

1. `A=A^T >= 0`;
2. `rank(A)=m-1`;
3. `ker(A)=span{z}` with `z>0` coordinatewise;
4. `R z=0`.

Choose any exact range solve

`A X = R^T`.

Define

`K := C-RX`.

As in T-P5-211/T-P5-180, `K` is independent of the kernel gauge in `X`, and

`(u,eta)^T H (u,eta)`

`= (u+X eta)^T A (u+X eta) + eta^T K eta`.

The new branch assumes only

5. `K=K^T >=0`,

with **no corank-one assumption on `K`**.

Because both terms are PSD, the zero-energy critical sheet is exactly

`u = alpha z-X eta`,

`eta in C_K := ker(K) ∩ R_+^p`,

with arbitrary `alpha in R`.

Thus the only new geometry is the nonnegative kernel cone `C_K`.

## 2. T212-A — PSD kernel cone extreme rays are principal corank-one supports

Let `K=K^T>=0` and

`C_K={eta>=0 : K eta=0}`.

For nonzero `xi in C_K`, let

`S=supp(xi)`.

Then `xi_S>0` and

`K_SS xi_S=0`.

### Theorem A

The ray `R_+ xi` is an extreme ray of `C_K` **iff**

**`dim ker(K_SS)=1`.**

Equivalently, every extreme ray of `C_K` is obtained by extending by zero a strict-positive generator of a PSD principal block of corank one.

### Proof: `=>`

Suppose `dim ker(K_SS)>=2`. Choose

`h_S in ker(K_SS)`

not proportional to `xi_S`, and extend `h_S` by zero outside `S`.

Since `K>=0`,

`h^T K h=h_S^T K_SS h_S=0`.

For a PSD matrix, zero quadratic energy implies membership in the kernel, hence

`K h=0`.

Because `xi_S>0`, replace `h` by `h-c xi` for a suitable real `c` strictly between two distinct ratios `h_i/xi_i`. The new `h` remains in `ker(K)`, is supported in `S`, is nonzero, and has both positive and negative coordinates on `S`.

Therefore for sufficiently small `eps>0`, both

`xi+eps h >=0`,

`xi-eps h >=0`,

and both lie in `ker(K)`. They are not proportional to `xi`, while

`xi = ((xi+eps h)+(xi-eps h))/2`.

Hence `R_+xi` is not extreme. Contradiction.

### Proof: `<=`

Assume `dim ker(K_SS)=1`. Suppose

`xi=u+v`,

with `u,v in C_K`.

For every coordinate outside `S`, `xi_i=0` while `u_i,v_i>=0`, so

`u_i=v_i=0`.

Thus `u,v` are supported in `S`, and

`u_S,v_S in ker(K_SS)=span{xi_S}`.

Nonnegativity forces both coefficients to be nonnegative. Hence `u` and `v` lie on the same ray as `xi`, so the ray is extreme.

QED.

### Consequence: cross-block equations are automatic

If `K>=0` and `K_SS xi_S=0`, then the zero extension `xi` satisfies

`xi^T K xi=0`.

PSD therefore implies

`K xi=0`,

including all rows outside `S`. Thus a strict-positive corank-one principal kernel support does **not** need a separate floating cross-residual tolerance: exact PSD already upgrades the principal zero to a full-kernel zero.

## 3. T212-B — support-descent decomposition without an abstract kernel basis

The cone `C_K` is polyhedral because it is cut out by the linear equalities `K eta=0` and inequalities `eta>=0`. Since it lies inside the nonnegative orthant, it is pointed.

A standard polyhedral theorem therefore says that it is the conic hull of finitely many extreme rays. In this PSD setting one can prove the required decomposition directly by support descent, which is more useful for formalization.

### Support-descent lemma

Every nonzero `eta in C_K` is a finite conic combination of vectors `xi^(r)` such that

- `xi^(r)>=0`;
- `K xi^(r)=0`;
- on `S_r=supp(xi^(r))`, `dim ker(K_{S_r S_r})=1`.

### Constructive proof

Start with nonzero `eta in C_K` and `S=supp(eta)`.

If `dim ker(K_SS)=1`, stop.

Otherwise choose `h_S in ker(K_SS)` not proportional to `eta_S`. As above, PSD upgrades its zero extension to an element of `ker(K)`. Replace `h` by `h-c eta` so that `h` has both positive and negative entries on `S`.

Define the maximal positive step sizes

`t_+ := min_{h_i<0} eta_i/(-h_i) >0`,

`t_- := min_{h_i>0} eta_i/h_i >0`.

Then

`eta^+ := eta+t_+ h >=0`,

`eta^- := eta-t_- h >=0`,

both remain in `ker(K)`, and each loses at least one support coordinate. Moreover

`eta = [t_-/(t_++t_-)] eta^+ + [t_+/(t_++t_-)] eta^-`.

Induct on support size. The process terminates in at most `p` support levels, yielding only support-minimal rays. By Theorem A those terminal rays are exactly the principal corank-one kernel rays.

This gives a concrete mathematical reason that T-P5-211's corank-one machinery remains useful even when full `K` has high corank: the higher-corank cone decomposes into finitely many lower-support corank-one pieces.

## 4. Exact rational generator packet

Assume now that `K` has rational entries.

For every extreme support `S`, `K_SS` is a rational matrix with a one-dimensional real kernel. Rational row reduction therefore produces a nonzero rational kernel vector. Since the real extreme generator is strict positive on `S`, orient the rational generator so that all its support coordinates are positive.

Hence every extreme ray admits a rational generator. After arbitrary positive rational rescaling, collect one generator per distinct extreme ray as columns of

`V=[xi^(1) ... xi^(r)] in Q^{p x r}`.

Then

**`C_K = {V y : y>=0}`.**

No pseudoinverse, eigenvector, square root, irrational nullspace basis, or tolerance is required.

A checker need not trust that a submitted support is an extreme ray merely because the producer says so. For a proposed column `xi` with support `S`, the exact local packet is:

1. `xi_S>0`;
2. `K_SS xi_S=0`;
3. `rank(K_SS)=|S|-1` (or any equivalent nonzero rank-revealing minor plus determinant zero);
4. `K_SS>=0` inherited from `K>=0`.

Theorem A supplies extremality. Global completeness of a submitted list of rays is a separate finite cone-coverage obligation; do not silently infer it from a partial list.

## 5. T212-C — common-zero debit residual becomes a zero-loaded-ray filter

Partition the debit matrix as in T-P5-211:

`Q = [[P,T^T],[T,U]]`.

Let the reference common-zero state be

`x_*=(z,0)`.

Assume the T-P5-210 hypotheses:

- `Q` is copositive;
- `x_*>=0`;
- `x_*^T Q x_*=0`.

Because `z>0` on the active support, the standard copositive-zero contact argument gives

**`Pz=0`,**

**`Tz>=0`.**

Proof: active coordinates allow both positive and negative infinitesimal perturbations while staying in the orthant, so their first derivative must vanish; inactive coordinates only allow positive perturbations, so their first derivative must be nonnegative.

Define the T-P5-211 pullback quantities

`g := Tz-X^T Pz = Tz >=0`,

`G := U + X^T P X - T X - X^T T^T`.

For a zero-`H` tangent

`v(alpha,eta)=(alpha z-X eta, eta)`, `eta in C_K`,

the debit first-order term is

`(Qx_*)^T v(alpha,eta)=g^T eta`.

Thus the T-P5-210 bi-critical condition is exactly

**`g^T eta=0`.**

Write `eta=Vy`, `y>=0`, and define the generator loads

`b:=V^T g`.

Since `g>=0` and every column of `V` is nonnegative,

`b>=0`.

Therefore

`g^T eta=b^T y=0`

iff

**`y_j=0` for every generator with `b_j>0`.**

So higher corank does not create a coupled first-order equality. It merely filters the kernel rays.

Let

`J0 := {j : b_j=0}`

and let `V0` be the submatrix containing only those zero-loaded rays. Then every bi-critical reduced-kernel direction is exactly

**`eta=V0 y`, `y>=0`.**

This is the second structural compression:

**arbitrary high-corank nonnegative kernel cone -> finite zero-loaded extreme-ray cone.**

## 6. T212-D — second-order debit reduces to one orthant quadratic

Because `z^T P z=0` and `Pz=0`, the debit expansion on the `H`-zero sheet is

`v(alpha,eta)^T Q v(alpha,eta)`

`= 2 alpha g^T eta + eta^T G eta`.

On the bi-critical cone `g^Teta=0`, the signed active parameter `alpha` disappears completely:

**`v(alpha,eta)^T Q v(alpha,eta)=eta^T G eta`.**

Now substitute `eta=V0 y`, `y>=0`, and define

`M0 := V0^T G V0`.

Then the high-corank second-order search is exactly

**find `y>=0` such that `y^T M0 y>0`.**

Equivalently, without using any additional inherited positivity,

**a positive-debit witness exists iff `-M0` is not copositive.**

This is already a direct bridge back to the existing P5 copositivity machinery.

## 7. T212-E — under the T-P5-210 copositive-debit assumption, even the copositivity search disappears

There is an additional simplification specific to the actual T-P5-210 branch.

### Lemma

`M0` is automatically copositive.

### Proof

Take arbitrary `y>=0`, let `eta=V0y`, and choose for example `alpha=0`. Then

`v=(-Xeta,eta)`

is a tangent direction at `x_*=(z,0)`. Since `z>0`, the active coordinates of

`x_*+eps v`

remain nonnegative for sufficiently small `eps>0`; the inactive coordinates are `eps eta>=0`.

Because `Q` is copositive,

`(x_*+eps v)^T Q (x_*+eps v)>=0`.

The constant term is zero because `x_*` is a `Q`-zero. The first-order term is zero because `eta` uses only zero-loaded rays. Therefore

`eps^2 eta^T G eta>=0`,

hence

`y^T M0 y>=0`.

QED.

### Exact existence criterion

For a symmetric copositive matrix `M0`,

`exists y>=0 : y^T M0 y>0`

iff

**`M0 != 0`.**

Indeed, if some diagonal entry is positive, use the corresponding coordinate vector. If all diagonal entries vanish but `M0` is nonzero, copositivity forces every off-diagonal entry to be nonnegative; a positive off-diagonal entry then gives a positive value on `e_i+e_j`.

Therefore, in the full T-P5-210 assumptions, the high-corank dual-critical decision is simply:

1. enumerate/cover the zero-loaded extreme rays of `C_K`;
2. form `M0=V0^T G V0`;
3. if `M0=0`, there is no positive second-order debit witness on this reduced-kernel cone;
4. if `M0!=0`, a positive second-order debit witness exists.

This is exact finite algebra after the extreme-ray coverage packet is trusted.

## 8. Corank-one T-P5-211 is recovered exactly

Suppose `K` has corank one and its nonnegative kernel is a strict positive ray generated by `xi`.

Then `V=[xi]`.

The load is

`b=xi^T g`.

Because `adj(K)=kappa xi xi^T` for some `kappa>0`, T-P5-211's first scalar is

`Bcrit=g^T adj(K) g=kappa (xi^Tg)^2`.

Thus

`Bcrit=0`

iff

`b=0`.

When that holds,

`M0=[xi^T G xi]`.

T-P5-211's second scalar is

`Ccrit=tr(adj(K)G)=kappa xi^T G xi`.

Hence

`Ccrit>0`

iff

`M0>0`.

So T-P5-212 is not a competing criterion. It is a strict higher-corank extension whose one-ray specialization is exactly the T-P5-211 two-scalar packet.

## 9. Exact rational regression — raywise checks miss a cross-ray witness

This regression shows why high corank cannot be handled by running T-P5-211 independently on each extreme ray and checking only diagonal second-order scalars.

Take one active coordinate and two inactive coordinates. Let

`A=[0]`,

`R=[0;0]`,

`K=[[0,0],[0,0]]`.

Thus `z=1`, `X=0`, and the endpoint matrix at the candidate threshold is simply

`H=0`.

The reduced kernel cone is

`C_K=R_+^2`,

with two extreme rays

`xi^(1)=e1`, `xi^(2)=e2`.

Let the debit matrix be

`Q = [[0,0,0],
      [0,0,1],
      [0,1,0]]`.

For every nonnegative state `(x0,x1,x2)`,

`x^T Q x=2 x1 x2>=0`,

so `Q` is copositive. The reference state

`x_*=(1,0,0)`

is a `Q`-zero, and

`g=0`.

Therefore both extreme rays are zero-loaded.

The pulled matrix is

`M0=[[0,1],[1,0]]`.

Each ray separately has zero second-order debit:

`e1^T M0 e1=0`,

`e2^T M0 e2=0`.

So a naive raywise scalar test would report no positive witness.

But their combination gives

`(e1+e2)^T M0 (e1+e2)=2>0`.

Hence the tangent

`v=(0,1,1)`

is an exact T-P5-210 second-order witness.

If one embeds this at ray parameter `t_*=1` by taking

`M(t)=(1-t)Q`,

then `M(t)` is copositive for `0<=t<=1`, while every `t>1` fails on `(0,1,1)`.

This regression proves:

**in higher corank, zero-loaded extreme rays must be recombined through their full cross-quadratic matrix `M0`; diagonal/raywise tests are incomplete.**

## 10. Exact zero-loaded filter regression

Keep `K=0`, but take a common-zero copositive debit with

`g=(1,0)`

and no second-order energy on the second ray. Then

`b=(1,0)`.

The first kernel ray is not bi-critical and must be discarded; only `e2` survives. A producer that ignores the first-order load and combines both rays can create a false second-order candidate. Thus `b_j=0` filtering must occur **before** the quadratic `M0` test.

This is the high-corank counterpart of T-P5-211's `Bcrit=0` gate.

## 12. Boundaries deliberately left open

### Boundary A — active block `A` has higher corank

This review keeps T-P5-211's strict-positive corank-one active anchor. If `ker(A)` has dimension greater than one, `Rz=0` for one contact vector does not imply full range compatibility, and the signed active zero sheet gains additional kernel directions. Route that branch to T-P5-178/T-P5-186-style range/kernel machinery; do not apply the one-dimensional active formula here.

### Boundary B — `K` is not PSD

The principal-support theorem uses PSD twice: zero principal quadratic energy must lift to the full kernel, and the zero-`H` sheet must split termwise. Copositive-but-indefinite `K` requires a different one-sided zero-cone analysis.

### Boundary C — incomplete generator list

Verifying each proposed extreme ray locally does not prove that all extreme rays were found. Missing a zero-loaded ray can create a false SAFE conclusion. The producer must supply a complete support enumeration or a separate cone-coverage theorem.

### Boundary D — source `Q` not independently known copositive

Then `M0` need not be copositive, so `M0!=0` is not enough. Fall back to the exact generic criterion `-M0` not copositive for existence of positive energy, or directly provide a positive rational witness `y`.

### Boundary E — non-rational source/candidate

The structural theorem is real-valued, but the exact-rational generator packet requires rational `K`. Algebraic/interval source binding remains a separate adapter problem.

### Boundary F — actual P5 source, path, runtime, and admission

No claim is made about actual same-key matrices, selector identity, cell/tube coverage, trajectory viability, Float64 interval semantics, P8/M4, Lean/kernel status, 封不觉 verification, admission, or registry state.

## 13. Recommended dispatcher handoff

For the higher-corank reduced-Schur branch after T-P5-211:

1. certify `K>=0` exactly;
2. enumerate principal supports `S` whose `K_SS` has a strict-positive one-dimensional kernel;
3. certify that these rays generate `ker(K)∩R_+^p`;
4. compute each first-order debit load `b_j=g^Txi^(j)`;
5. discard every `b_j>0` ray;
6. form `V0` from the zero-loaded rays and `M0=V0^T G V0`;
7. under inherited `Q` copositivity, decide the T-P5-210 second-order branch by exact `M0=0` versus `M0!=0`;
8. without inherited `Q` copositivity, use the existing copositivity layer on `-M0` or provide an explicit positive witness;
9. if a positive witness is found, combine it with the already-proved T-P5-210 endpoint theorem to obtain the local sharp-threshold obstruction;
10. keep all source/coverage/formal/admission gates external.

This is the smallest exact bridge I can see from T-P5-211's higher-corank boundary back into the existing P5 support/copotivity machinery without introducing a generic floating nullspace solver.
