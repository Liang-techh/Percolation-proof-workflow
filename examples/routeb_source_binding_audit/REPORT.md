# Route-B source-binding audit (bounded, read-only)

Date: 2026-09-05. Scope is only block (4,5), `M(q)`, `H0`, Christoffel terms,
and potential gradients. No trajectories, browser access, broad tests, or
external-project writes were performed. The source snapshots are under
`snapshots/original_target/` and `snapshots/current_exact/`; `SHA256SUMS.csv`
is the snapshot manifest.

## Exact/derived facts

1. **Exact: the deployed DH source equation.** In the original target
   `routeB_dense_Mq/dhport_lib.jl:31-43`, `fk_frames` builds the six DH
   transforms and stores each joint axis from the parent-frame third column.
   `:46-60` defines
   `M(q)=Σ_i [m_i Jv_i(q)^T Jv_i(q)+Jw_i(q)^T(R_i(q)(I_i/3)R_i(q)^T)Jw_i(q)] + 10^-6 I`.
   `:63-70` defines the COM gravitational potential. `:73-99` then defines
   `C_FD` from central differences of `M` and `G_FD` from central differences
   of the potential, with `h=10^-5`; `:102-109` solves the actual acceleration.

2. **Exact: the Fourier generator's intended algebra.**
   `routeB_dense_Mq/routeB_fourier_rational_probe.py:110-150` repeats the
   transform/parent-axis/COM/Jacobian construction in Gaussian-rational
   Fourier dictionaries and accumulates the same translational and direct
   `I_val/3` rotational terms. The analytic exporter
   `routeB_analytic_fourier_dynamics_probe.py:92-152` writes full mass,
   potential, Christoffel, and potential-gradient CSVs. Its own report text at
   `:199-215` calls this a parallel analytic interface and explicitly does not
   replace the finite-difference port.

3. **Derived exact: current CSVs agree with the current literal reference.**
   The current exact mass CSV has 610 data rows and the potential CSV has 17
   rows (`snapshots/current_exact/reference.json:4-11`). Summing the mass CSV
   at zero and adding `10^-6` to the diagonal gives the literal `M0` in
   `ReferenceMass.lean:14-20`, entry-for-entry. Computing
   `H0[i,j] = -Σ_n a_n ν_{n,i}ν_{n,j}` from the 17 potential rows gives the
   literal `H0` in `ReferenceMass.lean:22-28`, entry-for-entry. The nonzero
   entries are
   `H0[2,2]=-2029689/400000`, `H0[2,3]=H0[3,2]=-101043/80000`,
   `H0[2,5]=H0[3,5]=H0[5,2]=H0[5,3]=H0[5,5]=-20601/400000`, and
   `H0[3,3]=-101043/80000` (one-based physical axes).

4. **Exact: current Lean mass/potential objects are literal, not source-bound.**
   `ReferenceMass.lean:14-28` declares `M0` and `H0` as hard-coded matrices;
   `:37-43` proves only their symmetry. Its potential bridge at `:87-105`
   defines a four-coordinate `referencePotential` through
   `realFourierPotential`, then proves a remainder identity. It does not define
   or quantify a full `M(q)`.
   `PotentialSlice.lean:7-8` explicitly says its 17-row encoding makes no
   physical-DH identification; `:48-55` defines the Fourier potential and
   `:63-84` proves only a slice identity. `reference.json:4-8` independently
   records `Lean_compiled=false`, `physical_DH_identification_proved=false`,
   and `Float64_binding_proved=false` for that reference run.

5. **Exact but generic: Christoffel algebra is available.**
   `ChristoffelPower.lean:18-29` defines the tensor contraction convention and
   `:44-85` proves the finite-sum power identity. This accepts an arbitrary
   tensor `T`; it does not prove that the `T` supplied to Lean is the tensor
   generated by the source's finite differences of `M(q)`.

## Missing binding equations / theorem obligations

### B45-1 — full mass reconstruction (OPEN, highest priority)

Prove, for every real `q` in the declared angle domain and with explicit index
mapping, the source equation

```text
M_FD(q) = EvalFourier(routeB_fourier_mass_full_rational.csv, q)
           + (1/10^6) I_6
         = mass_matrix(q; regularization=1/10^6).
```

The CSV equality to `M0` at `q=0` is derived above, but it is not this
functional identity. The proof must bind DH transform order, parent-axis
choice, COM midpoint, `I_val/3` rotational convention, row/column order, and
the diagonal regularizer. Required source evidence:
`dhport_lib.jl:31-60`; Fourier generator `routeB_fourier_rational_probe.py:110-150`;
CSV writer `routeB_analytic_fourier_dynamics_probe.py:100-113`.

### B45-2 — potential reconstruction and gradient (OPEN)

Prove the functional identity

```text
U_DH(q) = EvalFourier(routeB_fourier_potential_rational.csv, q)
G_FD,k(q) = (U_DH(q+h e_k)-U_DH(q-h e_k))/(2h),  h=1/10^5.
```

The present Lean `realFourierPotential` is a literal 17-row cosine object and
the slice/remainder lemmas are valid for that object, but
`PotentialSlice.lean:7-8` leaves the DH identification open. The source
potential and finite-difference gradient are at `dhport_lib.jl:63-70` and
`:93-99`; the Fourier gradient export is at
`routeB_analytic_fourier_dynamics_probe.py:98-99,141-152`.

### B45-3 — `H0` meaning and gradient-at-zero (OPEN, with exact arithmetic already derived)

Bind the literal Lean matrix to the source/Fourier derivative statement

```text
H0[i,j] = ∂²U_DH/∂q_i∂q_j (0)
        = -Σ_n a_n ν_{n,i}ν_{n,j},
∇U_DH(0)=0.
```

The CSV arithmetic establishes the displayed values exactly, and the Lean
remainder use of `H0` is at `ReferenceMass.lean:93-105`; what is missing is the
functional/source semantic bridge from the DH potential to those CSV rows.
The current reference's `gradient_U0` is only a recorded literal (`reference.json:136-142`),
not a proof of the DH gradient identity.

### B45-4 — source Christoffel binding (OPEN)

After B45-1, prove that the tensor used by the Lean generic theorem satisfies

```text
C_FD,i(q,v) = Σ_j,k 1/2 (D_k M_FD[i,j] + D_j M_FD[i,k] - D_i M_FD[j,k]) v_j v_k
```

with `D_k M_FD` represented by the same central-difference operator as the
source (`dhport_lib.jl:73-92`). Then prove that the resulting contraction is
the source `Cdq` returned by `arm_MCG`; only the final algebraic contraction is
currently covered by `ChristoffelPower.lean:44-101`. The analytic CSV
generator computes a parallel exact derivative (`routeB_analytic_fourier_dynamics_probe.py:94-97`)
but does not bind it to the deployed FD output.

### B45-5 — block-(4,5) residual/model equation (OPEN; concrete mismatch to resolve)

The PMI source fixes `ja,jb=4,5`, loads only `M0` (`routeB_pmi_certificate.jl:46,85-89`),
and builds the block model

```text
f1=-a4*q4-c4*v4+kc*q5+gw4*w
f2=-a5*q5-c5*v5+kc*q4+gw5*w
```

at `:98-101`, while the actual DH acceleration uses `tau-Cdq-Gq` at
`dhport_lib.jl:102-109`. In particular, the PMI `kc=0.05` cross terms have no
corresponding term in the actual `tau` equation. A theorem must therefore
either prove an explicit decomposition/error equation, e.g.

```text
I_B f_B(q_B,v_B,w) - M0_BB a_B(q,v,w) = l_B(q,v,w),
```

with every remote-state, `C_FD`, `G_FD`, and `kc` contribution accounted for,
or replace the model by one that is definitionally the actual source. The
current exporter evaluates the same residual numerically at `:169-179` but
that is not a proof of the equation.
